# Product Knowledge: FRANK AI Operator

Use only these facts. If something is not here, say you will find out.

## What it is
FRANK (Lisbon, founded December 2024) builds an AI Operator for insurance brokers and agents.
It captures every client request on email, WhatsApp and documents, reads the documents, and does
the back-office work inside insurers' portals as a named user: multi-insurer quotes, renewals,
policy changes, claims follow-up. Nothing reaches the broker's client without a named person at
the broker pressing send.

## Proof (Portugal only)
- 350+ agencies and offices, 1,500+ users, about 70,000 back-office tasks a month.
- Agencies report about 10 hours of admin freed per person per week.
- Raised 2.9 million euros in October 2026 (Armilar, Start Ventures).

## UK status: be honest
- FRANK enters the UK in 2027. No UK clients, no UK case studies yet.
- No Acturis or OpenGI connector yet. The Operator works directly on insurer portals, so it sits
  alongside the broker system rather than inside it. Connectors for Acturis and OpenGI are planned.
