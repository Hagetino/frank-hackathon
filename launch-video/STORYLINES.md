# Storylines

References: `refs/` (the two Pinterest pins and the "Flow" image Lukas sent). What we take from them:
a pale, airy ground (#EEF0F4), one iridescent ribbon/glass object as the hero material (cyan → violet
→ pink → warm orange), light thin sans display with one italic word, and real UI cards set on a
curved wall that the camera travels along. FRANK's navy/teal identity is not required.

All three carry the same spine: **one fictional UK broker** goes from a line on the FCA register to a
booked €39 trial, and the header pill **"Humans involved 0"** never changes.

---

## A. "Zero" ★ recommended

Because it is the one a stranger follows: one broker, one path, one number that refuses to move.

- **Device / form:** the result travels / one continuous take. The iridescent ribbon from the "Flow"
  image *is* the pipeline; the camera rides it, and every station is a real piece of the app sitting
  on the ribbon's surface.
- **What you see and feel:** a single register line (Kingsmere Risk Solutions Ltd) slides off a list and becomes a glowing bead on
  the ribbon; the ribbon carries it through map, ranking, drafts, call and inbox while the counter
  stays 0. It feels calm and inevitable, like the work does itself.
- **First 2 s:** full-frame white, a row of the FCA register in tiny grey type; one row (the hero
  firm, Kingsmere Risk Solutions Ltd) turns ink-black, lifts, and the line *Every broker in the UK* types beside it. The
  ribbon sweeps in underneath and catches the row.
- **Beats:** hook (register row) → tension (the row multiplied into thousands, title *and nobody to
  call them*) → reveal (*FRANK Outreach Engine*, the ribbon folds into the header with the 0 pill) →
  proof: map → rank → write → call → reply → booked → payoff (pull back: 4 ribbons spread from UK to
  ES, BR, US; pill still 0) → claim (*Open a market. Not an office.*) → end card.
- **Look:** #EEF0F4 ground with 2% grain, Inter 300 at 120 px, one Instrument Serif italic word per
  title, the ribbon as the only colour; real UI cards in white with 24 px radius and a soft 0.12
  shadow.
- **Sound:** a soft, warm 100–110 BPM electronic bed; a glass "tick" on each station, a keyboard
  patter under the drafts, a real phone ring and the agent's first spoken line, a soft chime on
  "Booked".
- **Length:** 32–40 s. Each station is a self-contained shot, so dropping a feature drops ~3 s.

## B. "The wall"

- **Device / form:** type carries the argument / split screen to one. Straight from the second pin:
  a curved wall of real app cards rotates past the camera on the beat.
- **What you see and feel:** a tight kinetic opener (*New market. Same team.*), then the wall
  spins: each card is one feature at full size, cut on the beat, with a one-word verb (*Find. Rank.
  Write. Call. Close.*). At the end all cards collapse into one, the header with "Humans involved 0".
  Fast, confident, very "product drop".
- **First 2 s:** the word *New* in giant thin type, a map of the UK pours through its letters.
- **Beats:** hook (type) → tension (*Same team.* one person icon vs a growing list) → reveal →
  proof as 5 cards on the wall, one per beat → payoff (cards collapse to one) → claim → end card.
- **Look:** the same ground; cards wear iridescent mesh behind real UI, like the pin.
- **Sound:** 120 BPM, cuts on every beat, whoosh per card.
- **Length:** 25–30 s. Easiest to re-cut when features change (one card each), but closest to a
  "feature list", which is the main thing a launch film should avoid.

## C. "The call" (the bold one)

- **Device / form:** a moment in someone's day / one frame that rebuilds. The film is one phone
  call, heard in full, from the broker's side.
- **What you see and feel:** a UK broker's desk at 09:12, the phone lights up. We hear: *"Hi Sarah,
  I'm an AI assistant calling on behalf of FRANK…"*. While the call plays, the frame rebuilds behind
  the transcript to show what the agent already knew: the cluster, her score, the email she got on
  Monday, her renewal workload. It ends with her saying *"Go on then, Thursday works"*, the trial
  booking itself, and the pill: Humans involved 0. Surprising, human, slightly uncanny in a good way.
- **First 2 s:** a ringing phone card filling half the frame, caller line "FRANK (AI)".
- **Beats:** hook (ring) → tension (her objection "we've tried software, it never talks to our
  portals") → reveal (the screen behind the call) → proof (what the agent knew, station by station)
  → payoff (booked) → claim → end card.
- **Look:** same ground, transcript as large type, the ribbon only as the call's waveform.
- **Sound:** carried by the voice; needs the ElevenLabs voices (two: agent + broker). Music very low.
- **Length:** 40–50 s. Strongest for a pitch room, but it depends on row 6b (ElevenLabs) shipping.
